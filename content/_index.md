Hi, I am **Vikas Kumar**, an MA Economics student at **UPRTOU, Prayagraj**. My current interests are in **digital currencies, household finance, CBDC design, and behavioral economics**.

I am especially interested in how programmable money and payment system design can shape household decisions, welfare outcomes, and public policy delivery. I want this site to serve as a simple academic home for my research, writing, and professional materials.

## Research Interests

- Central bank digital currencies (CBDC)
- Digital payment systems
- Household finance
- Behavioral economics
- Public policy design
