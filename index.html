Vikas Kumar

Economist & Researcher · Varanasi, India

I study how programmable digital money reshapes household welfare — formalising the trade-off between targeting efficiency and individual autonomy. My current work introduces the Digital Envelope Model and the State λ Paradox, grounded in primary survey data from Varanasi and India's lived experience with demonetisation, UPI, and CBDC pilots.

My MA dissertation at UPRTOU, Prayagraj develops original theoretical frameworks for CBDC design, supervised by Dr. Harendra kumar (Assistant Professor). I am also a Research Assistant at Dev Indravati PG College, where I led fieldwork and empirical analysis on digital payment behaviour.
